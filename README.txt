## Project Overview
The Artisan Bean Coffee Roastery is a fictional specialty coffee brand based in Cape Town, South Africa. This website serves as a digital storefront for the business, showcasing their products, workshops, and brand story.

### Target Organisation
- **Type:** Small Business / Retail
- **Name:** The Artisan Bean Coffee Roastery
- **Industry:** Specialty Coffee Roasting & Retail
- **Location:** Cape Town, South Africa

---

## Website Goals and Objectives

### Primary Goals
1. **Increase Online Sales:** Drive whole-bean coffee sales through a clean, user-friendly product display.
2. **Promote Workshops:** Increase workshop bookings by 40% within the first quarter.
3. **Build Brand Authority:** Establish The Artisan Bean as a trusted specialty coffee roaster in the region.
4. **Engage Customers:** Provide clear, accessible information about the brand's story, products, and values.

### Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)
- Monthly unique website visitors
- Online order conversion rate
- Workshop enquiry form submissions
- Newsletter sign-up rate (future implementation)
- Average session duration

## Design and User Experience

### Colour Palette
| Colour | Hex Code | Usage |
|--------|----------|-------|
| Deep Brown | `#4A2C2A` | Headings, navigation background |
| Dark Brown | `#2C1810` | Footer, dark backgrounds |
| Warm Cream | `#FFF8F0` | Page background, card backgrounds |
| Burnt Orange | `#D4764A` | Buttons, accent elements |
| Muted Brown | `#E8DDD4` | Borders, subtle backgrounds |

### Typography
- **Headings:** Montserrat (sans-serif) — clean, modern, bold
- **Body Text:** Lora (serif) — elegant, readable, premium feel
- **Hierarchy:** Clear heading levels with consistent spacing

### Design Approach
- **Clean & Minimalist:** Focus on the product with plenty of white space
- **Warm & Inviting:** Coffee-inspired colours create a cosy, premium atmosphere
- **Card-Based Layout:** Products and team members displayed in cards for visual consistency
- **Responsive:** Mobile-first approach with breakpoints at 768px and 480px
