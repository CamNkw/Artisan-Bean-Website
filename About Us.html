About The Artisan Bean

Our Story

Founded in 2020 by former national barista champion, Mia van der Merwe, The Artisan Bean started as a small home‑roasting operation in her garage. Word spread fast, and within a year, we opened our first café in the heart of Gardens. Today, we supply whole‑bean coffee to over 20 local restaurants and host sold‑out workshops every weekend.

Mission

To source ethically grown, high‑quality coffee beans from around the world, roast them with precision, and educate our customers on the journey from bean to cup.

Vision

To become the region's most trusted specialty coffee roaster — known for transparency, exceptional quality, and genuine human connection.
